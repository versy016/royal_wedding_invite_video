# Gemini (Veo) prompt – opening video with an intact wax seal → `opening.mp4`

## How to use

1. In Gemini, choose **Video (Veo)**. If you have **Frames to video** (Gemini or Google Flow), use it:
   - **First frame:** `05_video_first_frame_closed_doors.png`
   - **Last frame:** `06_video_last_frame_garden.png`

   If only one image can be attached, attach the first frame.
2. Settings: **portrait 9:16**, longest duration available (8 s is fine), audio on.
3. Paste the prompt below as it is.
4. Save the result as `opening.mp4` in the project folder and in `wedding-site/`.
   The page's text timing will need re-syncing to the new video.

---

## PROMPT

> Animate the attached image. Portrait 9:16, one continuous cinematic shot, dreamy painterly
> storybook style that exactly matches the image: warm ivory and cream, antique gold, sage green,
> lavender wisteria, blush and white roses, golden-hour haze. Locked composition, slow and graceful
> motion only.
>
> 0–1 s: Very slow push-in toward the closed ivory double doors. The satin ribbon runs across both
> doors and is held in the centre by a round sage-green wax seal embossed with a gold "MS" monogram.
>
> 1–3 s: The wax seal stays perfectly whole, one single solid round piece. It gently peels free of
> the ribbon, lifts slightly toward the camera and swings up and away to the right in a soft arc,
> like a lid on a hinge. It tilts a little as it turns, so its smooth back catches the light. The
> monogram stays intact and legible, and the seal glides out of frame on the right. The seal never
> cracks, splits or breaks, and is never cut in half. Nothing of the seal stays on the doors.
>
> 3–4.5 s: With the seal gone, the ribbon loosens and its two ends slide softly down the doors and
> out of sight. The two doors swing inward and open. Warm golden light and soft rays pour through
> the widening gap.
>
> 4.5–8 s: The camera glides gently through the doorway into a sunlit garden: a wisteria archway
> with an ornate gold "MS" plaque at the top, a stone path leading to a tiered fountain, glowing
> lanterns and rose bushes. Several delicate white butterflies flutter slowly across the sky. End
> on a calm, steady shot of the garden (match the last-frame image) with the butterflies still
> drifting.
>
> Sound: soft romantic harp and strings, a gentle wax-lifting "tick", a soft door creak, faint
> birdsong. No voices.
>
> Avoid: cracked seal, broken seal, split seal, seal cut in half, two half-seals on the doors, wax
> fragments, extra or changing text, misspelled monogram, people, faces, hands, fast camera
> moves, flicker, morphing doors.
